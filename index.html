THE QUICK GUIDE

How to play

  • Roll a six to bring a token out of your yard.
  • Choose a glowing token to move it by the number rolled.
  • Land on an opponent to send them back—unless they're on a safe star.
  • Reach the center with an exact roll. First to bring all four tokens home wins!
  • Roll a six or capture a rival to earn an extra turn.
